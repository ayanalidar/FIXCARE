## Common Microwave Problems We Fix

Microwave faults are surprisingly consistent across brands — most fall into the heating circuit, the door interlock, the high-voltage section, or the control panel. Our technicians carry the parts to handle all four in a single visit, including the safety-critical door switches and the high-voltage capacitor. Here are the issues we fix most often in Kashmiri homes and small kitchens.

- **Microwave running but not heating** — The most common fault. Usually a failed magnetron, a blown high-voltage fuse, a faulty capacitor, or a burnt diode. We test the high-voltage section with proper discharge procedures before recommending any part.
- **Turntable not rotating** — Usually a worn turntable motor, a stripped coupling, or in some cases a misaligned drive shaft. We replace the motor with a genuine part and verify the rotation.
- **Sparking inside the microwave** — Caused by a damaged waveguide cover, food or grease build-up on the cavity walls, or a worn magnetron antenna. We replace the waveguide cover, clean the cavity, and verify the magnetron is safe to reuse.
- **Keypad or touchpad not working** — A failed membrane keypad, a damaged flex cable, or in some models a faulty control board. We replace the keypad as a sealed unit and test each button.
- **Door latch not engaging or microwave runs with door open** — A safety-critical fault. The door interlock switches are worn or misaligned. We replace the full interlock assembly and verify all three switches before returning the unit.
- **Magnetron failure and arcing noise** — A failing magnetron buzzes or arcs internally. We replace it with a genuine part rated for your specific model — substitutions cause uneven heating and shorter life.
- **Fuse blowing every time it runs** — A shorted high-voltage capacitor, a grounded magnetron, or a failed door switch. We isolate the short with a megger test before replacing anything, so the new fuse does not blow on first use.
- **Bad smell or smoke inside the cavity** — Usually food or grease burning on the waveguide cover, or in worst cases a failing magnetron. We chemically clean the cavity and replace the waveguide cover before testing.

## Why Choose Professional Microwave Repair

A microwave is one of the few household appliances that contains a genuinely high-voltage circuit — the magnetron operates at around 2,000 volts even after the unit is switched off, and the high-voltage capacitor can hold that charge for hours. The single most dangerous DIY mistake in Kashmiri households is opening the back cover and poking around inside a microwave that was just unplugged — the capacitor can deliver a serious or even fatal shock even with the power off. We always discharge the capacitor with a proper tool before any internal work.

The second common mistake is fitting a "compatible" magnetron that is not rated for the model. The microwave appears to heat, but the waveguide and cavity are not designed for that magnetron's output, leading to uneven heating, hot spots in the cavity, and a much shorter magnetron life. A failing magnetron can also leak microwave radiation if the door seal is worn — and only a properly equipped technician can test for that leakage. Microwave repair is one job where the savings of going to an unauthorized repairer are simply not worth the safety risk. Choosing a certified WeCare technician means your repair is done safely, with the right part, and with a warranty that means something.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My microwave runs but doesn't heat. What's most likely wrong?
In around 70% of cases the fault is a failed magnetron, a blown high-voltage fuse, or a faulty high-voltage capacitor. We test the high-voltage section — after safely discharging the capacitor — and replace only the part that has actually failed. The other 30% includes door switch faults and control board issues.

### Is it safe to use a microwave that sparks inside?
No. Sparking inside a microwave is usually caused by a damaged waveguide cover, food or grease on the cavity walls, or a failing magnetron antenna. Continuing to use the unit risks damaging the magnetron permanently or, in the worst case, starting a small fire inside the cavity. Switch it off and call us.

### How much does a microwave magnetron replacement cost in Srinagar?
A genuine magnetron replacement, including diagnosis and labor, typically ranges from ₹1,500 to ₹3,500 depending on the brand and the model. Samsung and LG magnetrons are at the lower end, Bosch and IFB convection magnetrons at the higher end. You always approve the quote before work begins.

### My microwave keypad doesn't respond. Can you fix it?
Yes, in almost every case. Membrane keypads fail from age and humidity — common in Kashmiri kitchens. We replace the keypad as a sealed unit with a genuine part and test every button. In some cases the fault is the control board behind the keypad rather than the keypad itself, and we diagnose that before recommending parts.

### Do you repair built-in convection microwaves?
Yes, including Bosch, Siemens, and Samsung built-in models used in modular kitchens. Built-in units are more complex to remove and refit, so we always send a technician with the right tools and a second pair of hands if needed. The repair cost is slightly higher than a counter-top microwave due to the installation work.
