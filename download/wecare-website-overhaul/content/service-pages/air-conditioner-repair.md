## Common Air Conditioner Problems We Fix

Most AC faults fall into four groups — cooling failures, refrigerant loss, water leakage from the indoor unit, and electrical or control issues. Our technicians arrive prepared with gauges, vacuum pumps, and the common spare parts so most repairs are completed the same day, even during the peak summer rush when the brand service centers are booked weeks out.

- **AC not cooling or low cooling** — Usually low refrigerant, a dirty evaporator or condenser coil, a weak capacitor, or a stuck compressor. We measure the pressures and the airflow before recommending anything.
- **Gas refill needed — R32 or R410A** — Common after 3-5 years of use. We find the leak first, weld or replace the affected tube, vacuum the system, and refill genuine refrigerant to the manufacturer's specified charge.
- **Water leakage from the indoor unit** — Most often a blocked condensate drain, a cracked drain tray, or an unlevelled indoor unit. We clear the drain, clean the tray, and check the installation level.
- **AC making noise from indoor or outdoor unit** — Indoor noise is usually a dirty blower or a failing cross-flow fan bearing. Outdoor noise is usually a worn compressor mounting, a bent condenser fan blade, or a loose housing screw.
- **Remote not working or AC not responding** — A dead remote, a faulty IR receiver on the indoor unit, or a failed control PCB. We test the remote with a camera, then the receiver, then the board.
- **Compressor tripping or not starting** — Usually a weak capacitor, a faulty overload protector, or in inverter ACs a failed inverter board. We test the compressor windings before recommending a replacement.
- **Bad smell when AC runs** — Bacterial or mould growth on the evaporator coil and the drain pan. We chemically clean the coil, sanitize the drain pan, and show you how to prevent recurrence.
- **AC running but no airflow** — A stuck cross-flow fan, a failed fan motor, or in some cases a frozen coil caused by low refrigerant. We diagnose the cause and thaw the coil before testing.

## Why Choose Professional AC Repair

The single most damaging DIY mistake we see in the Jammu region is a "gas top-up" without finding the leak — done by an unauthorized repairer with a portable cylinder and no gauges. The AC cools for a few weeks, the leak continues, and the undercharged compressor eventually burns out. The customer then pays for both a gas refill and a compressor replacement. A proper gas refill requires recovering the remaining refrigerant, finding and welding the leak, vacuuming the system to remove moisture, and refilling by weight to the manufacturer's specified charge — anything less is a temporary fix sold at a permanent price.

The second common mistake is using the wrong refrigerant. R32 and R410A operate at different pressures and require different service fittings and oil — mixing them or substituting one for the other damages the compressor and is unsafe. We carry only genuine, brand-traceable refrigerant and we recover and recycle the old gas rather than venting it. AC refrigerant handling also requires certification under the relevant J&K and central regulations, both for your safety and for the warranty on the unit. Choosing a certified FixCare technician means your AC is repaired correctly, with the right refrigerant, by someone who is accountable by name.
