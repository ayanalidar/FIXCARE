## Common Refrigerator Problems We Fix

Refrigerator faults usually fall into three categories — cooling failures, electrical failures, and physical leaks. Our technicians carry the parts to handle all three in a single visit, including the slow-to-source items like defrost timers and PCB modules for inverter fridges. Here are the issues we fix most often in Jammu homes.

- **Fridge not cooling but light still works** — Points to a thermostat failure, a stuck defrost timer, a choked evaporator coil, or in inverter fridges a control board fault. We test the compressor, the relay, and the defrost circuit before recommending any part change.
- **Freezer not freezing, fridge not cooling** — Usually a gas leak, a weak compressor, or a choked capillary. We locate the leak, weld it, vacuum the system, and refill genuine refrigerant to the manufacturer's specified charge.
- **Over-freezing in the fridge compartment** — Often a faulty thermostat or a stuck solenoid valve in frost-free models. We replace the thermostat and verify the compartment temperatures before leaving.
- **Water leaking inside or under the fridge** — Most often a blocked defrost drain, a cracked evaporator tray, or a damaged door gasket letting humid air in. We clear the drain, reseal or replace the gasket, and check the tray.
- **Compressor noise or constant running** — A failing compressor, a worn start relay, or a stuck condenser fan can all cause loud running and high power bills. We diagnose the cause and replace only what is actually faulty.
- **Ice build-up in the freezer compartment** — Usually a failed defrost heater, a faulty defrost sensor, or a damaged door seal. We replace the heater and defrost the unit fully before testing.
- **Fridge tripping the house power** — A shorted compressor winding, a failed overload protector, or a grounded defrost heater. We isolate the fault with a megger test before recommending a fix.
- **Door seal broken or not closing properly** — A common problem in older fridges in Jammu city and Samba. We replace the gasket with a genuine seal and check the door alignment so it does not return.

## Why Choose Professional Refrigerator Repair

A refrigerator is not a toaster — the sealed system holds pressurized refrigerant, and any work on that system requires both a certification and the right recovery equipment. The single most common reason we see for repeat fridge failures in the Jammu region is an unauthorized local repairer "topping up" gas without finding the leak. The fridge cools again for a few weeks, then fails again, and the customer has paid twice for a problem that was never actually fixed. By the time we arrive, the compressor has often been damaged by undercharging or by moisture that entered the system through an unvacuumed line.

The second common mistake is replacing the thermostat when the actual fault is a stuck defrost heater — the symptom is the same, but the part is different. Our technicians carry thermometers and test both compartments before recommending any part change. Freon and modern refrigerants like R600a and R134a are also environmentally controlled substances — handling them without proper recovery equipment is illegal and dangerous. Choosing a certified FixCare technician means your repair is done once, done correctly, and done with a warranty that means something.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My fridge is not cooling but the freezer is fine. What's wrong?
In a frost-free double-door fridge, this is usually a stuck defrost timer, a failed defrost heater, or a choked evaporator coil — the freezer stays cold but the cold air can no longer circulate down into the fridge compartment. We test the defrost circuit and the circulation fan before recommending any part.

### How much does a refrigerator gas refill cost in Jammu city?
It depends on the refrigerant type and the fridge capacity. R134a top-ups typically range from ₹1,800 to ₹2,800, R600a from ₹2,200 to ₹3,500, and full system recharge after a leak repair is higher. We always find the leak first — topping up gas without fixing the leak is a waste of your money and our reputation.

### Do you replace fridge compressors, and is it worth it?
Yes, we replace compressors with genuine branded units. For fridges under eight years old, a compressor replacement is usually worth it. For older fridges with multiple other worn parts, we will honestly tell you when a repair is no longer economical and recommend a replacement instead.

### My fridge makes a loud humming noise. Is the compressor dying?
Not always. A loud hum can also come from a worn start relay, an unlevelled compressor mounting, or a failing condenser fan. We test the compressor windings and the relay before recommending a replacement — many noise issues are fixed with a ₹600 relay, not a ₹6,000 compressor.

### Do you repair side-by-side and French-door refrigerators?
Yes. We have brand-trained specialists for Samsung, LG, and Whirlpool side-by-side and French-door models, including the inverter compressor variants. These fridges have more complex defrost circuits and electronic control boards, so we always send a specialist rather than a general technician.
