## Common Dishwasher Problems We Fix

Dishwasher faults usually fall into four groups — cleaning and wash-performance issues, water-circulation and drainage faults, door and seal problems, and electronic or error-code issues. Our technicians arrive prepared with the common spare parts and the manufacturer-specific diagnostic codes for Bosch, IFB, Samsung, LG, Siemens, and Faber models. Here are the issues we fix most often in Kashmiri homes and small commercial kitchens.

- **Dishes not cleaning properly or still dirty** — Usually a clogged spray arm, a worn wash pump, low water inlet pressure, or a faulty detergent dispenser. We descale the spray arms, test the pump, and check the inlet flow before recommending parts.
- **Dishes cloudy or with white film** — Hard water in much of Kashmir leaves mineral deposits on glassware and the heating element. We descale the unit, install or replace the integrated softener resin, and recommend the right detergent and rinse aid.
- **Dishwasher not draining or water left at the bottom** — A blocked drain pump filter, a jammed drain pump impeller, or a kinked drain hose. We clear the blockage, flush the pump, and test the drain cycle.
- **Error code on the display** — Bosch (E15, E24, E25), IFB (F8, E4), Samsung (5E, 1E, LE), LG (OE, 1E, AE) — each code points to a specific fault. We read the code, verify the underlying issue, and clear it only after the cause is fixed.
- **Door seal leak or water on the floor** — A perished door gasket, a damaged door hinge, or a misaligned door. We replace the gasket with a genuine part and check the hinge alignment so the leak does not return.
- **Spray arm not rotating or dishes in one corner still dirty** — A blocked spray-arm jet, a worn spray-arm bearing, or in some cases a failed circulation pump. We descale or replace the spray arm and verify rotation with a test cycle.
- **Detergent dispenser not opening mid-cycle** — A failed dispenser actuator, a stuck dispenser latch, or in some models a control board fault. We replace the dispenser as a sealed unit and test the mid-cycle opening.
- **Dishwasher not starting or no response** — A tripped internal fuse, a failed door interlock, or in some units a main control board fault. We isolate the fault before recommending parts to avoid repeat failures.

## Why Choose Professional Dishwasher Repair

A dishwasher is one of the more complex kitchen appliances — it combines a sealed water-heating circuit, a high-pressure circulation pump, a dosing system for detergent and rinse aid, a softener resin for hard water, and a delicate electronic control board in a single chassis. The single most common DIY mistake we see in Kashmiri households is using regular dish soap or excessive detergent, which overflows the dispenser, floods the floor, and can damage the rubber seals beyond repair. The second common mistake is forcing a stuck dispenser door with a knife, which snaps the latch and cracks the dispenser body.

Unauthorized local repairers are often unfamiliar with dishwashers entirely, since adoption in Kashmir is still relatively low — they treat it like a washing machine and replace parts at random, charging for a "new pump" when the actual fault was a blocked spray-arm jet. Hard water is the hidden factor behind a large share of dishwasher faults in the valley — without proper softener resin and a periodic descale, the heating element, the spray arm jets, and the pump internals all build up scale that gradually kills the unit. Choosing a certified WeCare technician means your dishwasher is diagnosed by someone who actually knows the model, repaired with the right part, and descaled to a standard that protects the rest of the machine.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My dishwasher leaves a white film on glasses. Can you fix this?
Yes. White film on glassware is almost always hard-water scale, not a detergent fault. We descale the dishwasher, check or replace the integrated softener resin, and recommend a rinse aid and the right detergent combination for your local water. We also offer a periodic descale service to keep the problem from coming back.

### My Bosch dishwasher shows an E24 or E25 error. What does it mean?
E24 and E25 codes on Bosch dishwashers point to a drain issue — usually a blocked drain pump filter, a jammed impeller, or a kinked drain hose. We clear the blockage, flush the pump, and test the drain cycle before clearing the code. The drain filter itself is a service item that should be cleaned every month — we will show you how.

### Do you repair commercial dishwashers in hotels and restaurants?
Yes. We service small commercial under-counter and hood-type dishwashers from Bosch, IFB, and Faber used in Kashmiri hotels, restaurants, and office canteens. Commercial units have higher-pressure pumps and shorter cycle times than residential models, so we always send a technician with commercial experience. We also offer priority commercial call-out to minimise downtime during service hours.

### How much does a dishwasher circulation pump replacement cost in Srinagar?
A genuine circulation pump replacement, including diagnosis and labor, typically ranges from ₹2,800 to ₹5,500 depending on the brand and the model. Bosch and Siemens pumps are at the higher end, IFB and Samsung pumps at the lower end. We always diagnose first and quote before any work begins — many "not cleaning" calls are actually a blocked spray-arm jet, not a pump failure.

### Do dishwashers work well with Kashmiri water, and do I need a water softener?
Most modern Bosch, IFB, and LG dishwashers have an integrated softener that handles moderately hard water. For very hard water in parts of the valley, an external pre-softener helps. We can test your inlet water hardness during a service visit and advise whether an external softener is worth it for your specific location.
