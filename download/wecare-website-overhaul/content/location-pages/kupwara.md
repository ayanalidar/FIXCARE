# Appliance Repair in Kupwara — Service Across Town, Handwara & the Lolab Valley

Kupwara is the northernmost district of the Kashmir Valley — a region of dense deodar and pine forests, apple and walnut orchards, and the famous Lolab valley that sits among the hills. The distance from Srinagar, the thick forest cover, and the long winters all shape how appliances work here. Refrigerators and freezers work overtime during the apple and walnut harvest because cold storage at the village level is rare; washing machines get pushed hard during the harvest season when farm clothes come in muddy and heavy; and microwave ovens do daily duty reheating noon chai and the leftover wazwan from weekend weddings.
