# About FixCare Service Center — Jammu's Trusted Local Appliance Repair Team

## Our Story

FixCare Service Center was founded to fill a gap that every household in the Jammu region has experienced: the choice between brand-authorized service centers that are slow, expensive, and often require transporting the appliance to a distant workshop, and unauthorized local repair shops that are unreliable, use duplicate parts, and offer no warranty once they leave. We started as a small team of two certified technicians in Jammu city with a simple commitment — arrive the same day, diagnose honestly, use only genuine spare parts, and stand behind every repair with a written warranty. Several years and 500+ repairs later, that commitment has not changed, but our team, our coverage, and our stock of brand-specific spare parts have grown to serve the entire Jammu region — from Jammu city to remote areas like Poonch and Doda.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to make reliable appliance repair accessible to every household in the Jammu region — regardless of where they live or which brand of appliance they own. We believe a broken washing machine should not mean a week of disruption, a faulty refrigerator should not mean wasted food, and a malfunctioning AC should not mean sleepless summer nights. We deliver on this by offering same-day service in Jammu city, next-day service across nearby districts, 2-day service to remote areas like Rajouri, Poonch and Doda, fair and transparent pricing that you approve before any work begins, and only genuine spare parts sourced from brand-authorized distributors in Jammu & Kashmir.

## Our Team

Our technicians are the heart of FixCare. Every member of our team is formally certified, brand-trained across Samsung, LG, Bosch, IFB, Whirlpool, Godrej, Haier, Voltas, and Panasonic, and background-verified before they ever enter a customer's home. They carry FixCare ID cards and arrive in branded service vans stocked with common spare parts. We invest in ongoing training every quarter so the team stays current on new models, updated error codes, and changing part specifications — important in an industry where a 2023 model appliance can have completely different diagnostics from its 2020 predecessor.
