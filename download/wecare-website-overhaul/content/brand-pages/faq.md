# Frequently Asked Questions — Appliance Repair in Kashmir

We have answered the questions our customers across Kashmir ask us most often — about how fast we reach, what we charge, the parts we use, our warranty, and how to book a repair. ## General Service

### How fast can a WeCare technician reach my home?

For Srinagar city, our standard response time is same-day — usually within 2 to 4 hours of booking during business hours. For other cities across the Kashmir Valley (Anantnag, Baramulla, Budgam, Pulwama, Ganderbal, Bandipora, Kupwara), we typically reach next-day. Emergency visits within 1 hour are available in Srinagar for an additional charge. When you book, we will give you an honest expected arrival window, not a vague "sometime tomorrow."

### Do you charge a visit or diagnostic fee?

We charge a nominal ₹200 to ₹300 visit fee depending on your location, which is fully adjusted against the final repair cost if you choose to proceed with the repair. The diagnostic itself is free — our technician will examine the appliance, identify the problem, and give you a written quote before any work begins. If you choose not to proceed, you only pay the visit fee.

### Are your technicians certified and trained?

Yes. Every WeCare technician is formally certified in the appliance categories they service, carries a WeCare ID card, and is background-verified. For premium brands like Bosch, Samsung, and LG, we have brand-specific trained specialists on the team. We also conduct internal refresher training every quarter to keep the team updated on new models, revised error codes, and changing part specifications.

### What areas of Kashmir do you cover?

We currently serve eight cities across the Kashmir Valley: Srinagar (same-day), Anantnag, Baramulla, Budgam, Pulwama, Ganderbal, Bandipora, and Kupwara (next-day). For Srinagar we cover all major neighborhoods including Lal Chowk, Hyderpora, Rajbagh, Karan Nagar, Sonwar, and Bemina. If your area is not on this list, call us anyway — we frequently serve nearby locations and may be able to help.

### Do you offer emergency or out-of-hours service?

Yes. We operate 24/7 including weekends and public holidays. For genuine emergencies — a fridge full of food at risk, an AC failure during a heatwave, a washing machine flooding — we can dispatch a technician within 1 hour in Srinagar for an additional emergency charge. Outside Srinagar, emergency response depends on technician availability in your area; call us and we will do our best.

## Repair & Warranty

### Do you use genuine spare parts?

Always. We source spare parts directly from brand-authorized distributors in Jammu & Kashmir. Every part we install comes with the manufacturer's own warranty plus our written warranty on top. We never use duplicate, refurbished, or aftermarket parts without your explicit prior consent. If a substitute part is ever proposed, it will be clearly disclosed on the quote before you approve it.

### What is your warranty period on repairs?

Our warranty ranges from 3 months for minor parts (belts, switches, thermostats) to 12 months for major parts (compressors, motors, magnetrons). Most standard repairs carry a 6-month warranty. The exact warranty period applicable to your specific repair is printed on your written warranty card at the time of service. See our full [Warranty Policy](/warranty-policy/) for details.

### What happens if the same problem recurs within the warranty period?

If the same fault recurs within the warranty period, we return and re-repair it free of charge — no visit fee, no labor charge, and no charge for the same replacement part. If the same part fails again after a second re-repair, we offer a full refund of the original repair cost. Just call us with your warranty card number and we will schedule a priority visit with the original technician whenever possible.

### Can you repair appliances still under brand warranty without voiding it?

Yes, in most cases. We follow the manufacturer's published repair procedures and use only genuine parts, which preserves your brand warranty. However, if your appliance is still within the brand's free warranty period, we recommend contacting the brand's authorized service center first to claim free repair. We step in when the brand warranty has expired, when the brand cannot reach you in time, or when you prefer faster local service.

### Can you complete the repair in the same visit?

For the majority of repairs, yes — our technicians carry common spare parts in their service vans so most jobs are completed in a single visit. For less common or model-specific parts that need to be ordered from Jammu or outside, we complete the diagnosis on the first visit, give you a written quote, source the part, and return to fit it within 2 to 3 working days. You are never charged extra for a return visit.
