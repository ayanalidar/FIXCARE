# Brands We Service — Certified Repair for All Major Appliance Brands

At FixCare Service Center, we have deliberately invested in building brand-specific expertise across the appliance brands most commonly found in homes across the Jammu region. Instead of being a single-brand service center, our technicians are cross-trained on Korean, American, European, Indian, Chinese, and Japanese brands so you only need one phone call no matter what is in your kitchen, laundry, or living room. Every brand we service is supported by a genuine spare parts supply chain, brand-specific diagnostic procedures, and a written warranty on the work we complete.

## Brand Cards

### Samsung

Samsung is the most common brand in homes across the Jammu region, known for feature-rich washing machines, French-door refrigerators, microwaves, and split ACs. Our technicians are trained on Samsung's digital inverter compressors, eco-bubble washing systems, and the latest SmartThings-enabled models. We stock common Samsung spare parts including drain pumps, thermostats, belts, and PCB assemblies for fast same-visit repairs.

### LG

LG is another Korean leader popular across the Jammu region, especially for direct-drive washing machines, inverter-linear refrigerators, and dual-inverter air conditioners. We service LG's full home appliance range with brand-specific diagnostics for common error codes such as IE, OE, and FE on washing machines, and our team is trained on LG's smart diagnosis app for faster troubleshooting on newer models.

### Whirlpool

Whirlpool is an American brand with a strong foothold in Indian kitchens, particularly for top-load and front-load washing machines and large-capacity refrigerators with 6th Sense technology. We repair Whirlpool appliances across the Jammu region, with experience on the brand's adaptive defrost systems, IntelliFresh refrigeration, and StainWash washing programs. Genuine Whirlpool parts are stocked for common wear components.

### Bosch

Bosch is a German premium brand favored for high-end dishwashers, front-load washing machines, and built-in appliances. Bosch repair requires precise diagnostic procedures and brand-specific tools, and we are one of the few local services in the Jammu region willing to work on Bosch dishwashers. We use only genuine Bosch spare parts and follow Bosch's published service bulletins to preserve any remaining manufacturer warranty.

### IFB

IFB is an Indian brand specializing in front-load washing machines, dishwashers, and microwaves — commonly chosen for apartment living across Jammu city. Our technicians are trained on IFB's aqua-energie, 3D wash, and steam programs, and we stock common IFB spares including drain pumps, heating elements, and door seals. We also service IFB microwaves and dishwashers across all served cities.

### Godrej

Godrej is a long-trusted Indian brand across the Jammu region, particularly for refrigerators and washing machines built for Indian voltage and water conditions. We service Godrej refrigerators including the cool-defrost and Pentacool ranges, and Godrej washing machines from semi-automatic to fully automatic models. Genuine Godrej compressors, thermostats, and timers are stocked for fast turnaround.

### Haier

Haier is a Chinese brand popular for budget-friendly refrigerators, washing machines, and small ACs across the Jammu region. We repair the full Haier home appliance range with experience on common Haier fault patterns including defrost timer failures on fridges and motor coupling wear on washing machines. Genuine Haier parts are sourced directly from the brand's authorized J&K distributor.

### Voltas

Voltas is a leading Indian brand for air conditioners, particularly split ACs and window ACs common in Jammu city homes and offices. We service Voltas ACs for cooling issues, gas refill (R32 and R410A), PCB failures, compressor replacement, and annual maintenance contracts. Our technicians carry genuine Voltas PCBs, capacitors, and refrigerant gas for same-day AC repairs.

### Panasonic

Panasonic is a Japanese brand known for durable refrigerators, ACs, and microwaves with reliable inverter technology. We service Panasonic appliances across the Jammu region with brand-specific diagnostics for inverter compressor faults on refrigerators and inverter AC issues. Genuine Panasonic spare parts, including inverter boards and sensors, are stocked or sourced within 2-3 working days for less common models.

## Why Brand-Specific Expertise Matters

Each appliance brand uses unique error codes, part numbers, software diagnostics, and repair procedures. A technician trained only on one brand will often misdiagnose or use incompatible parts on another. Our cross-brand training means we can read Samsung's 4E code and LG's OE code with equal confidence, identify Bosch-specific drain pump failures, and source the exact Voltas PCB your model requires. The result is faster diagnosis, fewer return visits, and a repair that lasts.
