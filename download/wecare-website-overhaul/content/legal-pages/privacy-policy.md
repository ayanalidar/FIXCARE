# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 2026

FixCare Service Center ("FixCare", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when you book an appliance repair, contact our team, or browse our website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, how long we retain it, and the rights you have over your own data. Our practices align with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 issued by the Government of India, and we also follow the principles of the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where they apply to your interaction with us.

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to all visitors to our website and to all customers who book an appliance repair with FixCare Service Center, whether by phone, WhatsApp, our online booking form, or our contact form. By using our website or booking a service, you consent to the collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this Policy, please do not use our website or book our services.

## 2. Information We Collect

When you interact with FixCare, we may collect the following categories of personal information:

- **Contact information:** your name, phone number, email address, and residential address.
- **Booking details:** the appliance brand, model, and serial number; the description of the fault you report; the preferred service time slot; and the assigned technician's name.
- **Service information:** diagnostic notes, photographs of the appliance or its internal components taken by the technician during the repair, parts replaced, warranty card number, and the final invoice.
- **Payment information:** the payment method used (cash, UPI, card, or bank transfer) and the last four digits of any card used. We do not store full card numbers or banking credentials.
- **Communications:** records of phone calls, WhatsApp messages, and emails exchanged between you and our team for the purpose of providing service and resolving disputes.
- **Website analytics:** your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring URL, collected through Google Analytics in an aggregated and anonymized form.

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, biometric data, or health information as part of our standard service process.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect for the following purposes:

- To schedule, deliver, and follow up on appliance repair services requested by you.
- To communicate with you about your booking, including SMS confirmations, technician assignment, and warranty card delivery.
- To process payments and issue invoices, including GST invoices on request.
- To honor our written warranty, including contacting you if a safety recall affects a part installed in your appliance.
- To improve the quality of our service through internal review and customer feedback.
- To send you marketing communications about seasonal offers or new service offerings, only where you have provided explicit consent. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any SMS or by emailing us.

We do not use your personal information for any purpose that is incompatible with the purposes for which it was originally collected.

## 4. Information Sharing

FixCare does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing or commercial purposes. We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

- **Service technicians:** your name, phone number, address, and appliance details are shared with the assigned technician to enable the repair visit.
- **Payment gateways:** transaction details are shared with our payment service providers (such as UPI gateways) strictly for processing the payment you authorize.
- **CRM and SMS providers:** your phone number and booking status are stored in our internal customer relationship management system and shared with our SMS gateway provider to send booking confirmations and warranty cards.
- **Legal compliance:** we may disclose your information if required to do so by law, by a court order, or by a lawful request from a government or regulatory authority.

All third parties with whom we share your information are bound by confidentiality obligations consistent with this Policy.

## 5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical, organizational, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. These safeguards include restricted internal access to customer records, encrypted transmission of payment data through PCI-DSS compliant gateways, and secure storage of digital service records. While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and to understand how visitors use our pages. We use Google Analytics to collect anonymized, aggregated information about visitor behavior, including pages visited, session duration, and general geographic location. We do not use third-party advertising networks, retargeting pixels, or social media tracking on our website. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting your ability to book a repair.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information held by FixCare:

- **Access:** you can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
- **Correction:** you can ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.
- **Deletion:** you can request that we delete your personal information once it is no longer required for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legal retention requirements.
- **Withdrawal of consent:** you can withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.
- **Grievance redressal:** you can raise a complaint about how your data has been handled.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@fixcareservicecenter.in. We will respond to your request within 30 working days of receipt.

## 8. Children's Privacy

FixCare Service Center does not provide services to individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a minor has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will promptly delete that information.

## 9. Retention of Information

We retain your personal information for the duration of any active warranty on a repair we have completed, plus an additional two years to allow us to honor warranty claims and respond to service complaints. Booking records and invoices are retained for tax and statutory compliance for seven years as required under Indian law.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. We will post the updated Policy on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Material changes that affect your rights will be communicated by SMS or email if we have your contact details on file.
